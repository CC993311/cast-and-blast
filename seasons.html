FishBound's Species Guide includes fish from across North America. A species may not occur in every state, and rules may vary by lake, river, management zone, season, or whether you are fishing in state or federal waters. Confirm the exact current rules with the agency before fishing.

For ocean and offshore fishing, federal rules may also apply. Check NOAA Fisheries recreational fishing information.

This finder links to official government sources. It does not invent or estimate size limits, bag limits, seasons, or license rules. Always follow the current official regulations for the exact water you plan to fish.
